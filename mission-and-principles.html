Mission

Summer Camp is the experiential culmination of a vibrant community. Together, we can explore new and ancient ways of connection, truly feel our individual and collective states of being, and create a space of intentional living. This is safe space to heal, grow, participate, and be present in the time we dedicate to ourselves and each other.

Principles

  1. ▣ Expression Finding ways to authentically express ourselves and embracing the full spectrum of human emotion.
  2. ◧ Balance Finding an equilibrium between our needs for rest and play, to fill our cups as needed. Encouraging co-creation and child-like wonder, and knowing that we also need moments for rest and regeneration.
  3. ◍ Community Creating a space where community members can deepen their relationships through shared interests, and experience a world where you’re encouraged to meet new people, try new things, and be inclusive of others who are doing the same.
  4. ⟡ Consent Enthusiastic consent is a must. Campers are comfortable asking for it and voicing their needs, knowing this is a safe space to do so.
  5. ◎ Presence Allowing participants to be present with themselves, with each other, and in turn, something greater.
  6. ⌬ Participation We build this space together. Campers come with the mindset that nothing gets done alone, and we lend a hand proactively.
  7. ◇ Nature Understanding our place within this natural world, and our responsibility to leave it better than we found it.
