Ongoing Activities

These run throughout camp — jump in anytime!

  • Scavenger Hunt — A casual all-day hunt: grab a list of items and adjectives, capture your finds in any artistic medium, and submit by end of day for all of camp to peruse.
  • Elimination — A camp-wide stealth game where everyone wears a named clothespin and secretly hunts their assigned target, inheriting new targets with each steal until one winner remains.
  • iConnect4 — Before Wordle, before Minecraft, there was Connect4: play the lifesize set all weekend and battle through the bracket to the iConnect4 Championship at the Talent Show.
  • Baby Dome — Baby enrichment optimization zone.
  • Riverside Baby Pool — Aquatic recreation, reconfigured to integrate with iCamp Mini requirements.
