Location + Time

Trout Creek Wilderness Lodge

Address: 36799 S. Dickey Prairie Road, Molalla, OR 97038

Directions: Google Maps will take you to the owner's house, keep driving past it and you'll see a sign for Trout Creek Wilderness Lodge on your left. Turn in there and check-in at the greeting table!

Dates: July 1 - July 6

Timing: Campers are welcome to attend for the full duration, or a shorter stay that includes at least Build (Weds, 7/1, arrive by 2pm latest) or Break (Sun, 7/5, until 5pm). All campers are required to attend the welcome meeting (Thurs 7/2 @ 5pm).

The gist

Camp Mission

Summer Camp is the experiential culmination of a vibrant community. Together, we can explore new and ancient ways of connection, truly feel our individual and collective states of being, and create a space of intentional living. This is safe space to heal, grow, participate, and be present in the time we dedicate to ourselves and each other.
