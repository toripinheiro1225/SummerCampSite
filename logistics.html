Dues

The fees below are a flat rate and include venue rental, housing, community-cooked dinners, access to the sauna and spa, activities, cleaning/trash removal, and all other production expenses. It does not include personal beverages, other consumables (you'll need to be entirely radically self-reliant here), breakfast, or lunch.

The amounts are listed as ranges because the final cost is determined after accommodations selections are made and final budget is in (i.e. the math gets mathed after camp, and that's when final dues are determined and when you'll be asked to pay them).
