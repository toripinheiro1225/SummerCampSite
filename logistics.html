Dinner is provided!

Dinner is provided every evening (Wed–Sun). While we can't guarantee no cross-contamination, our cooks do their absolute darndest to accommodate dietary needs. Campers are responsible for bringing their own breakfast, lunch, and bevvies (alc and non-alc).

  • Sun: Return of Trash Dinner

Storing your food

Consistent with last year, the lodge fridges will be prioritized for communal dinner ingredients. Exceptions happily made for folks with specific dietary needs (reach out to Jenna with any questions). Otherwise, please plan to store any perishables (or cold bevvies) you bring in your own coolers. The closest grocery store (for ice runs, etc.) is located in Molalla, about 15 minutes away.

Prepping your food

Lodge kitchen is fair game for preparing your own breakfast and lunch — just be mindful of dinner crews' needs in the evenings. Please note that the kitchen is no longer stocked with oils or spices — so be sure to bring what you need. There will be some communal snacks. If you have any you want to share, let them pile abundantly in the main lodge! There's also a new Breville Espresso machine — ooo!

Water

Most of the water at Trout Creek is NOT POTABLE. There is one built-in water filter in the main lodge sink and large Berkey water filters in the main lodge and sauna area. The Berkey filters are very slow, so if you find them low, please refill them. Consider bringing a large jug of water for personal use.

Dues and Lodgings

The fees below are a flat rate and include venue rental, housing, nightly dinner, access to the sauna and spa, activities, cleaning/trash removal, and all other production expenses. You are responsible to BYO breakfast, lunch, drinks, and anything else you'd like. If it is not on the above list it will not be provided this year.

The amounts are listed as ranges because the final cost is determined after accommodations selections are made and final budget is in (i.e. the math gets mathed after camp, and that's when final dues are determined and when you'll be asked to pay them).

Fees (these INCLUDE the deposit you already paid)

  • Camping or Personal RV Camping: $425–475 per person
  • Glamping Tent (2 people, 1 or 2 Queen Beds): $525–575 per person
  • Cabin (2 people, 1 Queen Bed): $650–700 per person
  • Jupe (2 people, 1 Queen Bed): $650–700 per person
  • Solo Glamping Tent (1 person, 1 Queen Bed): $650–700 each

🏕️ You can find examples of housing on the Trout Creek Wilderness Lodge site under the "Accommodations" tab.
