Roles Snapshot

For newbies: roles are how we all contribute to making camp a knock-your-socks-off event. There is something for everyone. Feel free to ask questions. Sign up for a role

  • We Haul-It!Transport all camp gear between Seattle and camp, coordinating trailer loading, hauling, and unloading.
  • Builder UppersPlan and lead the setup of core camp structures and support others building installations.
  • Breaker DownersCoordinate teardown, cleanup, and packing to return camp to its original state.
  • SoundscapersSet up, move, and manage sound systems across camp while monitoring volume and troubleshooting issues.
  • LightmakersInstall and maintain lighting across key spaces to ensure visibility and atmosphere.
  • Leave No TraceOrganize and enforce cleanup efforts so the site stays tidy and is fully restored at the end.
  • ConciergeManage camper intake, housing, parking, and orientation to keep arrivals organized.
  • GiftersProduce and run the Saturday Gift Show (a talent-optional talent show), coordinating performers, setup, and flow of the event.
  • Decor DivasDesign and install themed decorations to shape the visual identity of camp.
  • Fluffer GnomesKeep shared spaces comfortable, cozy, and hazard-free throughout the week.
  • Fin-DomsManage the budget and guide financial decisions to keep spending controlled.
  • Lewks LoversCreate and maintain a dress-up space with styling support and shared costume pieces.
  • Camp ConnectorsDesign moments and systems that help campers meet, interact, and build connections.
  • HistoriansCapture and document camp through photos, video, audio, or other creative formats.
  • Chez CampPlan, cook, and coordinate meals for the group, including prep and kitchen cleanup.
  • Safety CrewResponsible for first aid and safety at camp.
  • Baby CampCoordinate and facilitate delightful environments and activities for the babies at camp, their parents, and their friends.

Art + Activities

Camp art and activities are created by campers, for campers <3 If you want to experience it, you can make it happen! Big or small, day or night, we're here to help you bring your art, activity, event, or offering to life. This is your opportunity to create. All skill levels are welcome and encouraged. Grants will be available to fund this work (exact numbers TBD, but in the past we have offered $100 grants). Some ideas to get the creative juices flowing:

Make Art:

  • installation
  • performance piece
  • sculpture
  • visual art
  • digital art
  • audio/sound art
  • film
  • ETC

Organize an activity:

  • Claytime/Mud Hut
  • Figure drawing
  • Birding walk
  • Philosophy discussions
  • Death cafe
  • Tea-party in the woods
  • Improvisational dance/movement session
  • Naked (or clothed!) yoga
  • River float
  • Tiki bar day party in the river
  • Collaborative cooking class
  • Literally anything you want
